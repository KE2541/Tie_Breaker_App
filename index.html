How The Tiebreaker works

1. Structure
Turn a vague decision into explicit options and factors.
2. Compare
See trade-offs side by side instead of relying on one-dimensional pros and cons.
3. Challenge
Surface missing information, assumptions, risks and questions worth investigating.
4. Decide
Use the analysis as a thinking aid. The final decision stays with you.
